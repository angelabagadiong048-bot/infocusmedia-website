02 How we work

Four steps. No mystery.

1

Brief.

One call. We ask direct questions about your business, your buyer, and your budget. If we’re not the right studio for it, we’ll say so.

2

Scope.

You get a fixed scope, a timeline, and a price before anything starts. No line items appearing halfway through.

3

Make.

Small crew, tight schedule. You see progress as it happens, not three weeks later.

4

Deliver.

Files organised, formatted per channel, ready to publish. Plus everything raw, if you want it.
